Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Tosoko is a private moments journal for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and where it stays. In short: your moments stay on your device, and we do not collect them.

Information you create

Photos you take or import, voice notes you record, the thoughts you write or dictate, the moods you pick and the place names you type are stored only inside the Tosoko app container on your device. Tosoko has no account system and no server. Your moments are not uploaded, synced, sold or shared with us or with anyone else.

Permissions

  • Camera is used only when you tap Take Photo, to capture a photo for a moment.
  • Photos read access is used only when you tap Import from Photos. Add access is used only when you tap Save to Photos to export a rendered card. Tosoko never changes or deletes photos in your library.
  • Microphone is used only while you hold to record a voice note or while you dictate.
  • Speech Recognition is used only when you tap Dictate. Recognition runs on your device when supported. The text appears as a suggestion and is saved only if you tap Insert.
  • Notifications: Tosoko asks for permission once on your first visit to the timeline. This version does not send any notifications.

Tosoko does not track you across apps or websites, does not request tracking permission and does not read the advertising identifier.

You can change any permission at any time in the Settings app on your device. Declining a permission never blocks the rest of the app.

Purchases

Moment Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. Tosoko keeps a local record of verified transaction identifiers on your device so that each purchase is added only once.

Analytics and third parties

Tosoko does not include analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The open-source libraries used to build the app run locally and do not collect personal data.

Data security and retention

Your data is kept in the app's private storage and protected by iOS data protection. It stays until you delete a moment, use Clear All Data in Settings, or delete the app. Clear All Data removes every moment, photo copy, voice note, draft, sample and remaining credit from Tosoko.

Children

Tosoko is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Tosoko Support page.

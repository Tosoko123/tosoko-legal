Support

Answers to common questions about Tosoko.

How do I capture a moment?

Tap the plus button, take or import up to four photos or skip that step, then hold to record a voice note or write a thought. Pick a mood, add a place if you like, review, and render.

Why does rendering use a credit?

Each render creates a finished 1080 x 1350 card on your timeline. Exporting that card to Photos again or saving text-only edits does not use another credit. Canceled or failed renders never use a credit.

Where are my moments stored?

Only inside Tosoko on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app or using Clear All Data removes your moments and remaining credits.

I denied camera, photos or microphone access

Open the Settings app, find Tosoko, and turn the permission on. You can always import photos instead of using the camera, or type instead of recording.

A purchase did not show up

Keep Tosoko open for a moment while connected to the internet. Verified purchases that were interrupted are added automatically the next time the app starts. Purchases are handled by Apple, who can also help with refunds.

Still need help?

Use the Support link on the Tosoko App Store page and describe what happened. Please do not include personal photos or private details.
